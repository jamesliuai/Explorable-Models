## Title and purpose

- Title: Where a line crosses zero
- Purpose: Predict, then explore, how the slope and the intercept place the root of y = m·x + b.

## Stages

| Stage | Instruction | Host-owned response request |
| --- | --- | --- |
| predict | Look at the line. Before anything else is shown, predict where it crosses y = 0. | Prediction: Where does this line cross y = 0, and why? |
| explore | Change the slope and the intercept and watch the root move. Try a slope of 0. | — |
| closed | This activity is closed. | — |

## Accessible alternatives

- Values and rule: the values panel and the rule state the slope, the intercept, the probe point and the root that the
  graph draws.
- Exact entry: every control accepts an exact typed value.
